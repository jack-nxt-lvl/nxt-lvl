Cancellations, returns & refunds

Clear remedies when plans change or an order goes wrong.

This policy covers future approved orders paid by card once processing is available. It does not enable payments, establish product eligibility, or replace rights and terms applicable to existing orders. Separate written terms are needed before any future cryptocurrency acceptance.

Cancel before dispatch

Full refund before carrier handover, local-delivery dispatch or pickup collection. No cancellation or restocking fee.

Problems with an order

For a verified damaged, incorrect or missing item, choose a replacement or refund, subject to lawful supply and availability.

Refund handling

Approved refunds are initiated within five business days, sooner where required. Bank posting time is separate.

Withdraw a research request

A pending research request may be withdrawn at any time. No payment is collected at that stage and no cancellation fee applies.

Cancel before dispatch or handover

Contact support with your order number. If cancellation is received before the parcel is handed to the carrier, before local delivery is dispatched, or before a pickup is collected, we will cancel the order and refund the full amount paid, including tax and fulfillment charges. There is no cancellation or restocking fee. Address or fulfillment changes require confirmation and, where necessary, a new research review.

If we cannot fulfill an order on time

If we cannot ship by the promised dispatch date, we will notify you before that deadline and offer a revised date or cancellation with a full refund for the unshipped order. We require your express agreement to a revised date. If we cannot obtain it, we will cancel and refund the unshipped order rather than treat silence as consent. We will also contact you if a confirmed local-delivery or pickup arrangement cannot be met. If we cannot fulfill the order at all, we will cancel it and refund the amount paid. For partial fulfillment, we will refund the unfulfilled items and related charges without imposing a new shipping charge on the portion already supplied.

Damaged, incorrect or missing orders

Contact support promptly, preferably within seven calendar days of delivery or discovering a missing parcel. Include your order number, a description, and available photographs of the packaging, label and lot identification. Later reports are still reviewed; this requested reporting period does not remove applicable rights. Do not use a material whose condition or identity is in doubt.

After reviewing the issue, a verified damaged, incorrect or missing item will be replaced at no additional charge or refunded, at your choice, subject to lawful supply and availability. A refund includes the affected item's price, applicable tax and related delivery charge. If the entire order is affected, the full order payment is refunded. We cover any return shipping we require for our fulfillment error and do not deduct processing or restocking fees. We will work with the carrier on missing parcels; you do not have to resolve our carrier claim before contacting us.

Change-of-mind returns and material handling

We do not accept change-of-mind returns or exchanges of research materials after dispatch or handover, including unopened materials, because custody and storage conditions cannot be verified after delivery. This does not exclude remedies for damaged, incorrect, defective, missing or otherwise nonconforming goods, or any rights required by law.

Never return research materials without written authorization and appropriate shipping instructions. Do not mail returns to the Wyoming business address without authorization. Support will confirm whether a return is necessary and provide an appropriate return address and handling instructions.

Refund timing and method

We initiate approved refunds within five business days. For a cancellation or refund required by applicable shipping rules, the clock runs from when the refund becomes due, not from a later internal approval, and any shorter applicable deadline takes priority. Card refunds are sent through the original payment method; bank posting time is separate and may vary. We provide confirmation when the refund is submitted and help trace a refund that does not appear. Store credit is never substituted for an owed monetary refund unless the customer chooses it. If the original refund route fails, we will arrange an appropriate verified alternative without removing applicable refund rights.
