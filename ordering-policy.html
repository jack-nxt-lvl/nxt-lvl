Shipping, delivery & pickup

Shipping requests are limited to United States destinations pending review of lawful supply and the organization address. A shipping option in the request form does not establish eligibility or confirm dispatch. Any shipping method, dispatch timeframe, fee and delivery estimate must be supplied before payment is made.

South Florida delivery and pickup require separate confirmation of coverage, appointment, authorized collector, charges and location. Do not travel or send money based only on a request. Same-day delivery and walk-in pickup are not promised.

If an accepted paid order cannot be shipped within the promised period, contact support for the revised timing or cancellation and refund options. Applicable cancellation and refund rights are not waived by the research-use restriction.

Cancellations, returns & refunds

Pending requests: you may withdraw a research request by contacting support with its reference. No payment has been collected at this stage.

Existing paid orders: contact support with the order reference to request a cancellation or report a missing, damaged, incorrect or disputed item. Keep the packaging and relevant lot details. Do not return chemicals or ship materials to an unconfirmed address.

Before future payment: the applicable cancellation conditions, return eligibility, return procedure, refund conditions and timing must be provided in writing. The full paid-order policy is being finalized; online payment remains unavailable during that process. No blanket “all sales final” rule is asserted here.
