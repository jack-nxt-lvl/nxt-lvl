Material reference

Tirzepatide research overview

Tirzepatide was described in discovery research under the identifier LY3298176 as a dual GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonist. Receptor identity and molecular characterization are useful when distinguishing it from other incretin-related research compounds. A shared 10mg vial label does not establish equivalent materials.
