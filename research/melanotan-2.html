Material reference

Melanotan-2 research overview

Melanotan II, also written Melanotan-2, is described in chemical research as a cyclic heptapeptide. Its ring structure and amino-acid stereochemistry are part of its molecular identity. A listing in the same melanocortin research family does not establish that another peptide is the same material.

  • Ask for identity evidence that matches the intended cyclic peptide and its stereochemistry.
  • Keep Melanotan-2, Melanotan-1 and PT-141 identities separate when reviewing labels, analytical reports and purchase records.
Are Melanotan-1 and Melanotan-2 just different vial amounts?

No. They are separate named materials. The “1” and “2” distinguish the listings, while the 10mg label describes the amount stated for each vial.
