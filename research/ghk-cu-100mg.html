Material reference

GHK-Cu 100mg research overview

This listing states 100mg of GHK-Cu material per vial. GHK-Cu is the copper complex associated with the tripeptide glycyl-histidyl-lysine in the cited fibroblast research. The reference chemistry is separate from the amount, composition and assay evidence for a particular stock lot.
