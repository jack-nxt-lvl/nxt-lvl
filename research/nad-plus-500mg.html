Material reference

NAD+ research overview

NAD+ denotes the oxidized form of nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide, a coenzyme involved in electron-transfer chemistry. It is chemically distinct from the reduced form NADH and from phosphorylated NADP compounds. It is not a peptide, even when supplied in a dry vial format.

What to verify for this material

  • Confirm the oxidized NAD+ form and the supplier’s salt, counterion and water-content specification.
  • Match the 500mg label to the offered lot and ask how the stated amount was determined; related coenzyme names are not interchangeable.
Is NAD+ a research peptide?

NAD+ is a nucleotide coenzyme. The freeze-dried research-material collection includes both peptides and other materials, so supplied format should not be used as a chemical classification.
