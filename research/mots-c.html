Material reference

MOTS-c research overview

MOTS-c was described as a 16-amino-acid peptide encoded within a short open reading frame of mitochondrial 12S rRNA. The original work investigated cellular metabolic signaling and experimental models. The mitochondrial reference describes the peptide’s discovery, not the manufacturing origin of a supplied vial.
