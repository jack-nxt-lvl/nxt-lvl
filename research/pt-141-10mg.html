Material reference

PT-141 research overview

PT-141 is described in primary research as a synthetic alpha-MSH analogue acting at melanocortin receptors, including MC3R and MC4R. This places it in a research family that also contains other structurally distinct peptides. Receptor-family membership is not proof that the materials are interchangeable.
