Material reference

Semaglutide research overview

Semaglutide is an acylated GLP-1 analogue. Its discovery publication describes amino-acid substitutions and a modification at lysine 26, with laboratory characterization of receptor and albumin binding. These structural features distinguish the reference molecule from native GLP-1 and other named analogues.

How does its research classification differ from tirzepatide?

The cited semaglutide work concerns a GLP-1 analogue. Tirzepatide research characterizes a distinct dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist. These are separate molecular identities, not alternate names for the same material.
