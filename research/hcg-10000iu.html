HCG 10,000 IU research overview

This HCG presentation is labeled 10,000 IU per vial. Structural work characterizes hCG as an alpha/beta glycoprotein heterodimer. A larger IU label does not by itself describe the protein mass, purity, glycosylation profile or manufacturing source of a supplied sample.
