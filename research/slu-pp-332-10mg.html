Material reference

SLU-PP-332 research overview

SLU-PP-332 is a synthetic research compound characterized as an agonist of estrogen-related receptors ERRα, ERRβ and ERRγ. The original study examined receptor-dependent cellular and animal responses. These nuclear-receptor references should not be confused with the conventional estrogen receptors.
