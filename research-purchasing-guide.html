1. Separate material identity from vial quantity.

A listing combines several different facts: the material name, the labeled amount in one vial, its supplied format and the number of vials in your selected pack. A larger pack changes the vial count; it does not change the stated amount in each vial.

For example, the GHK-Cu 50mg listing states 50mg per vial. A three-vial pack contains three separately labeled vials of that listing. The 100mg listing is a different catalog item. These amounts describe supplied material and are not doses.

Keep units exactly as listed. Do not substitute a volume in mL for an amount in mg, or treat an IU-labeled material as an equivalent mass. Request clarification when the material specification is incomplete.

A blend in one vial and a package of two separate vials are different products. Check each component and its labeled amount; a shared order does not establish compatibility or suitability for combined use.

2. Compare total price and average price per vial.

Quantity savings apply to the pack you select: two vials save 2%, three or four save 5%, five through nine save 8%, and ten save 12%. Increasing the number of an existing cart line repeats that selected pack. Choose a larger pack to receive its quantity rate.

3. Review the documents for the offered lot.

  • Match the material name and lot number on the label to the report.
  • Check the analytical method and what was measured. A reported result for one characteristic is not a claim about every other characteristic.
  • Confirm that the supplied format, quantity and storage documentation meet your laboratory’s requirements.
  • If a report is missing, mismatched or unclear, ask for clarification before payment.

A listing, image or certificate alone does not establish purity, sterility or suitability for a particular experiment. Read the COA review guide and open available batch reports. Product images are illustrative packaging previews.

4. Check fulfillment and the final total.

Standard U.S. shipping is $12 and becomes free when the discounted merchandise subtotal exceeds $200. Exactly $200 does not qualify. The overnight option is $30 and confirmed South Florida delivery is $25; those options become free above $475. Appointment-only pickup is free.

Dispatch normally takes 1–2 business days after acceptance and payment; carrier transit time is additional. Confirm overnight availability, a local delivery address or a pickup appointment before paying.

Research materials and packages do not include water. If your laboratory needs it, bacteriostatic water is sold separately and is optional in the cart. Review its suitability and documentation independently.

Review payment availability and the final total in checkout, along with the final-sale policy. Read the ordering guide before sending a payment.
