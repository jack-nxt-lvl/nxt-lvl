Best Direct-to-Wallet Routes

Use Swaps.app first. BitPay and Paybis are backups.

All three can deliver crypto to the final receiving wallet. Checkout uses Swaps.app first because it compares live routes and carries the exact crypto amount into its receive flow.

Recommended · best overall Swaps.app from checkout

Compares 9+ providers, shows the all-in amount before payment, and settles to the receiving wallet. A lower-verification route may appear when the customer and order qualify.

Backup · address field BitPay web Buy screen

Supports BTC, ETH, and USDT purchases to a final wallet address and compares partner offers. The recipient address can be filled directly on the Buy screen.

Backup · direct wallet Paybis

Offers card and Apple Pay purchases to an external wallet, including USDT on Ethereum. Paybis may also appear as one of the provider choices inside Swaps.app.

About verification: Swaps.app can show a lower-friction provider, but no legitimate card service can promise no verification. A purchase around $1,000 may still require ID, bank approval, or a security review.

Avoid scams: use the official app or website, ignore unsolicited “support” messages, and never give anyone your recovery phrase, password, verification code, or remote access to your phone.
!
Pause before you press SendThese four checks prevent the most common beginner mistakes.
  • Never share your recovery phrase or private key. NXT LVL and a legitimate payment provider do not need them.
  • The network must match exactly. A wrong-network transfer may be permanent and unrecoverable.
  • Crypto payments are generally irreversible. Check the first and last characters of the address and send only once.
  • Unsure? Stop before sending. Contact support and keep the checkout open so the payment details do not get mixed up.
