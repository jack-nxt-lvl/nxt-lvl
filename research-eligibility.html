WHO THIS IS FOR

Qualified laboratory and institutional research.

Requests must come from authorized purchasers acting for research organizations. The organization, research purpose and delivery destination must be independently verifiable. Requests may be declined if eligibility or lawful supply cannot be established.

We do not accept requests for personal experimentation, human or veterinary consumption, administration, diagnosis or treatment, or resale for those purposes. An acknowledgment does not override these restrictions.

Prepare these details

  • Organization identity and public contact information
  • Your role and purchasing authority
  • Intended laboratory work and requested quantities

What the review does—and what it cannot establish

Does checking the research-use box establish eligibility?

No. It records your acknowledgment. NXT LVL may request evidence of organization identity, purchasing authority and intended laboratory use before dispatch. Crypto checkout does not require a private approval code. Requests that cannot be fulfilled lawfully are handled under the cancellation and refund policy.

Does a COA establish suitability for human use?

No. Analytical reports relate to the samples and tests shown. They do not establish safety, sterility, medical suitability, or regulatory approval. Confirm the report and lot correspond to the proposed material.

Can I pick up or request delivery locally?

We ship across the USA. Local delivery and pickup are available only in the Miami and Fort Lauderdale areas. The same research eligibility requirements apply. Appointments, collector identity, location, fees and timing must be confirmed before payment or travel. Local inquiry details.

Is NXT LVL approved by Stripe?

No approval is represented on this site. Card acceptance remains unavailable unless the actual business and catalog are accepted by the provider and the required setup is complete.

Will you help me choose something for personal health goals?

No. We can answer material-identity, documentation, request-status and fulfillment questions. We do not recommend these materials for personal use or provide dosing or administration instructions.
