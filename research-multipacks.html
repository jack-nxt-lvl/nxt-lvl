Multipack questions

What is included?

The selected number of vials of the single material shown on the card, at its stated amount per vial. No solvent, administration supplies or additional compounds are included. Pack quantities do not describe a treatment course.

Will every vial come from the same lot?

Do not assume a single lot. If your laboratory requires a matched lot, confirm that requirement and request the applicable documentation before payment.

How does shipping or local delivery work?

U.S. standard shipping is $12; overnight is $30 when available. Pickup is free by appointment; confirmed South Florida delivery is $25. All available fulfillment options are free on merchandise totals above $475, after discounts. Exactly $475 does not qualify. Dispatch is normally 1–2 business days after acceptance and payment; transit time is additional. Confirm local availability before paying.

How do I order and pay?

Select a quantity to review the product and price in the shop, then add it to your cart. Crypto checkout accepts BTC, ETH or USDT; use only the address and network displayed by checkout. Read the payment instructions and final-sale policy before payment.

Can I choose a different quantity?

Yes. Open a material's detail page for all available pack sizes. For a larger or mixed-material purchase, request a quote with your exact requirements. Availability and documentation must be confirmed.
