NXT LVL Research is operated by BLUE LIGHT VENTURES LLC, 30 N Gould St, Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, United States. This notice describes the website features and research-review workflow. Contact NXT LVL Research at payment@nxtlvl-research.com or 754-290-7210 with privacy questions.

Information you provide

A research request includes your name, organization email, phone, role, organization name and public website, organization address, intended laboratory work, selected materials, quantities, fulfillment preference, and research-use acknowledgment. These details are used to evaluate eligibility, respond to the request, prevent misuse and keep a review record. Submitting a request does not subscribe you to marketing.

Do not submit identity-document scans, payment-card numbers, medical or patient information, wallet secrets, or confidential study data. If further verification is necessary, support will explain an appropriate method first.

Website and browser storage

The website stores cart selections in local browser storage and draft checkout contact details in session storage. An active payment session, where available, may also be retained for recovery in the current browser tab. Clear the website’s browser storage to remove these local copies. Approval codes are not deliberately saved in browser storage.

The hosting service receives ordinary connection information such as IP address, device/browser data and requested URLs. The research request service uses a hashed network identifier for short-term rate limiting. Google Fonts may receive connection information when fonts load.

Service providers and external links

Vercel hosts the website and server functions. The research-review record is stored through the configured Redis/Upstash database service. Resend delivers request notifications and transactional email. If enabled, Twilio delivers transactional text messages with the relevant consent. The AI assistant sends the messages you submit to OpenAI to generate a response; keep personal or sensitive information out of that chat.

Clicking a WhatsApp link or sending an email/text uses the selected provider’s service. A local WhatsApp inquiry prepares a draft for you to review before sending. Payment providers, if enabled, receive the details needed for their hosted checkout under their own privacy notices. Visiting an external funding service is separate from submitting a request here.

Retention and access

Research-request database records are configured to expire after 90 days from creation or the last review update. Temporary rate-limit records expire after two hours. Approval codes expire after seven days. Support correspondence, payment records and provider logs follow their separate operational and legal retention needs; deleting a request record does not automatically delete those records or a sent email.

Access to review records requires private server credentials. There is no public request lookup or self-approval page. Authorized reviewers use the information to assess the request and may contact an organization independently to verify purchasing authority.

Questions, corrections and deletion requests

Contact support to request access, correction or deletion of information you supplied. Include a request reference if available. We may need to verify your authority and retain information required for existing transactions, disputes or legal obligations. Do not send additional sensitive documents before a verification method is agreed.

This website is for authorized organizational research purchasers, not personal-use shopping. The published cancellation and refund policy covers future approved card-paid orders. Product eligibility, payment-provider review and required account setup must be completed before new payments are enabled. This notice will be updated when the website’s processing changes.
