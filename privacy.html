Information you provide

Checkout collects your name, email, phone, U.S. address, selected materials, quantities, fulfillment preference and research-use acknowledgment. These details are used to prepare your payment instructions, verify payment, review eligibility, arrange fulfillment and provide support.

When you choose a crypto payment option, your contact and order details are sent to order support, even if you do not complete payment. This is an order-support notification, not a marketing subscription.

A separate research review may request your role, organization name, public website, organization contact details and intended laboratory work. We use this information to assess purchasing authority, eligibility and lawful supply.

Do not submit identity-document scans, payment-card numbers, medical or patient information, wallet secrets, or confidential study data. If further verification is necessary, support will explain an appropriate method first.

Website and browser storage

The website stores cart selections in local browser storage and draft checkout contact details in session storage. An active payment session, where available, may also be retained for recovery in the current browser tab. Clear the website’s browser storage to remove these local copies. The crypto research-use acknowledgment and checkout details may be retained locally with an active payment so it can be resumed. Private approval codes from the separate optional review process are not deliberately saved in browser storage.

The hosting service receives ordinary connection information such as IP address, device/browser data and requested URLs. The research inquiry and crypto checkout services use hashed network identifiers to limit repeated requests and prevent abuse. Google Fonts may receive connection information when fonts load.

Service providers and external links

Vercel hosts the website and server functions. Research-review and payment-verification records are stored through Redis/Upstash. Resend delivers checkout-started notifications, research-request notifications and payment-confirmation emails. If enabled, Twilio delivers transactional text messages with the relevant consent. The AI assistant sends the messages you submit to OpenAI to generate a response; keep personal or sensitive information out of that chat.

Clicking a WhatsApp link or sending an email/text uses the selected provider’s service. The local inquiry form prepares a draft in WhatsApp, text messages or email; it does not itself submit an inquiry to NXT LVL. Opening that draft shares its contents with the selected app or provider. Review it before sending. Paybis and other external crypto services handle information entered on their own sites under their own privacy notices. NXT LVL does not collect card details in its checkout.

Crypto payment information

Payment verification uses the selected asset and network, receiving address, amount, order reference, transaction identifier and blockchain confirmation status. Public blockchain services are queried to check transactions. Blockchain transfers and wallet addresses are publicly visible; NXT LVL cannot erase a transaction from the blockchain.

Order references and transaction identifiers are linked in the payment record to prevent the same transfer from being credited to multiple orders. Payment-related support and confirmation emails can include your contact, fulfillment and order details. We never need your private key or wallet recovery phrase.

Retention and access

Research-request database records are configured to expire after 90 days from creation or the last review update. Temporary rate-limit records expire after two hours. Approval codes expire after seven days. The 90-day research-request period does not apply to payment-verification records. Those records are retained separately to prevent payment reuse and support transaction reconciliation. Support correspondence, payment emails and provider logs follow their separate operational and legal retention needs; deleting a research request does not automatically delete those records or a sent email.

Access to review records requires private server credentials. There is no public request lookup or self-approval page. Authorized reviewers use the information to assess the request and may contact an organization independently to verify purchasing authority.

Questions, corrections and deletion requests

Contact support to request access, correction or deletion of information you supplied. Include a request reference if available. We may need to verify your authority and retain information required for existing transactions, disputes or legal obligations. Do not send additional sensitive documents before a verification method is agreed.

This website is for authorized organizational research purchasers, not personal-use shopping. The published cancellation and refund policy applies to orders under its published effective date and terms, including direct crypto orders. Card processing is currently unavailable. This notice will be updated when the website’s processing changes.
