YOUR CHECKOUT, STEP BY STEP

Five clear steps.

01

Build your cart

Select a material and pack size, then tap Add to cart. Open the cart, check quantities and choose Checkout with crypto.

02

Enter your details

Choose U.S. shipping or confirmed local fulfillment. Enter your name, email, phone and full U.S. address, including for pickup. Read and accept the research-use and ordering terms, then continue to crypto payment.

03

Choose your crypto

Use Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), or Tether (USDT) on the network listed below. If you need to acquire crypto, any separate provider handles its own eligibility checks, fees and availability.

04

Send the exact amount

Use the current payment screen’s receiving address and exact crypto amount, or its QR code. Check the asset, network, address and quote timer. Include any wallet or provider fees so the correct amount reaches the receiving address. Send only once.

05

Return for confirmation

Keep or reopen your payment screen. Checkout checks the blockchain; a pending transaction is not confirmed payment. If needed, paste the transaction ID using the manual verification option. Keep your order reference and receipt.

Before you send

All sales final once an order is placed. No change-of-mind cancellations, returns, exchanges or refunds. Applicable legal rights remain. Read the policy and required exceptions.

  • Use the address from your current checkout, never an address sent by an unsolicited message.
  • Do not send on an expired quote. If already sent, verify that payment instead of paying twice.
  • Crypto transfers are generally irreversible; a refund requires a separate transaction under the refund policy.
  • Never share a recovery phrase, private key or one-time security code with support.
  • Checkout does not calculate sales tax. Contact support to confirm any applicable tax before payment.
