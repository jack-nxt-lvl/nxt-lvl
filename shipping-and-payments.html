Read before sending

Four rules that prevent the most common mistakes

Never share your recovery phrase or private key. NXT LVL and legitimate payment providers do not need either one.
Use only the network printed in checkout. Wrong-network transfers may be permanent and unrecoverable.
Check before approving. Crypto payments are generally irreversible, so confirm the amount and address and send only once.
Stop if anything looks different. Keep the checkout open and contact support before sending if the coin, network, amount, or address does not match.
